# ⌨️ Fullerene on split keyboards

Fullerene uses six columns per hand on three letter rows: the main 28 keys,
plus the four rarest keys (`q z ' ;`) on the outer pinky column. Every split
keyboard below has those keys; the rest (number row, bottom row, thumbs) is
left to your keymap.

> [!NOTE]
> The images show each keyboard's key positions, not exact drawings: key
> wells are drawn flat, and the case outlines are approximate.

## ErgoDox EZ

The outer pinky keys sit on the 1.5u outer column.

![Fullerene on the ErgoDox EZ](images/keyboards/ergodox-ez.svg)

## Kinesis Advantage360

The outer pinky keys sit on the 1.25u outer column, at the bottom of the key
well.

![Fullerene on the Kinesis Advantage360](images/keyboards/advantage360.svg)

![Fullerene on the MoErgo Go60](images/keyboards/go60.svg)

## Cyboard Imprint

[The Imprint](https://cyboard.digital/products/imprint) is made to measure,
so key positions vary from board to board. Shown here: the full 5x6 (function,
number and letter rows) with the Dual Arcs thumb cluster.

![Fullerene on the Cyboard Imprint](images/keyboards/imprint.svg)
